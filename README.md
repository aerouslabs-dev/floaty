# ⛵ Floaty

> **Organize your daily flow.** A cute, modern, and minimalist task & team management mobile application designed to keep your workflow smooth. Developed with a cute UI/UX aesthetic by **Aerous Labs**.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [https://floaty.bolt.host](https://floaty.bolt.host)

---

## ✨ Features

* **🎨 Cute & Modern UI/UX:** Clean, minimalist design featuring soft color palettes, customized paper boat branding, and splash screen from Aerous Labs.
* **🔐 Flexible Authentication:** 
  * **Google Sign-In:** Quick access using your Google account.
  * **Guest Mode:** Local storage-based offline sync so your data stays private on your device without cloud dependency.
* **📊 Dashboard & Monthly Review:** Track your tasks (Done, In Progress, Ongoing, Awaiting Review) at a glance.
* **📅 Interactive Calendar View:** Seamlessly manage daily schedules, ongoing tasks, and timelines.
